# A/B Test Plan

**Budget:** 50-100 ฿/วัน/ad set (ทดสอบ 7-14 วัน)
**Audience:** Lookalike 1% จาก buyer list + Interest targeting
**KPI:** CTR > 1.5%, CPC < 8 ฿, CPM 80-150 ฿

| # | Product | Test A | Test B | Audience | Why Test |
|---|---------|--------|--------|----------|----------|
| 1 | AI Prompt Pack | V1 Pain | V3 Curiosity | Office workers 25-45, ChatGPT interest | Pain vs curiosity hook |
| 2 | Obsidian Student | V1 Pain | V2 Social Proof | นักศึกษา 18-25, Notion/study apps | New tool needs trust |
| 3 | Pricing Calc | V1 Pain | V3 Curiosity | Freelancer 22-40, Upwork/Fastwork | Underpricing pain hits hard |
| 4 | Cold Email | V2 Outcome | V3 Curiosity | Sales/BD 25-45, B2B SaaS | Outcome metric is strong |
| 5 | AI Automation | V1 Pain | V2 Social Proof | SME owners 30-55 | Time-saving pain universal |
| 6 | CV Template | V1 Pain | V3 Curiosity | Job seekers 24-35, EN proficient | Rejection pain very real |
| 7 | N8N Pack | V1 Pain | V3 Scarcity | SME 30-55 + tech-savvy | Cost comparison drives action |
| 8 | Content Calendar | V1 Pain | V3 Curiosity | Creators/SME marketing 22-40 | Engagement mystery sells |
| 9 | Finance Tracker | V1 Pain | V2 Social Proof | Freelance + พนักงาน 25-45 | Bestseller social proof works |
| 10 | AI Agent Starter | V1 Pain | V2 Outcome | Devs 25-40, Python/AI interest | Speed-to-running is the win |

**Expected Benchmarks (Thailand B2C/B2B):**
- CTR: 1.2-2.5% (good), 3%+ (excellent)
- CPC: 5-12 ฿ (target <8)
- CPM: 80-180 ฿
- CVR (landing → sale): 1.5-3.5%
- ROAS target: 3x ภายใน 14 วัน

**Scaling rule:** ถ้า ROAS > 3x ใน 7 วัน → เพิ่ม budget 30% ทุก 3 วัน ห้ามเพิ่มเกิน 50% ต่อครั้ง (กระทบ algorithm)
